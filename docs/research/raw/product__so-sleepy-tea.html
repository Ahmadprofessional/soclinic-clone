SO Sleepy Tea is a soft, floral blend crafted to help you unwind and settle into the quieter moments of your day or night. A calming base of camomile offers a naturally smooth and mellow sip, while passion flower adds a delicate earthiness that rounds out the cup. Hints of lavender bring a serene aroma, and rose petals finish the blend with a touch of elegant sweetness. Whether you’re easing out of a busy day, creating a peaceful bedtime ritual, or simply craving a comforting herbal brew, this blend invites you to slow down, breathe deeply, and enjoy a moment of stillness
